title: Collaborate with Your Team
description: Invite members, assign ownership, and keep team execution aligned

Good collaboration in Kaneo comes from clear ownership and consistent project conventions.

## 1. Add people to the workspace

In your workspace:

1. Open **Members** (also under **Settings -> Workspace -> Members**).
2. Click **Add people**.
3. Type a name or an email address. Matching accounts appear as you type.
4. Pick a person, choose their workspace role and click **Add**. They are in the workspace right away, without an invitation. They get a notification in Kaneo and, when the server sends email, an email with a link to the workspace.
5. If nobody matches and you typed a full email address, choose **Send invitation to ...** instead. The person gets an invitation by email. If the server does not send email, copy the link from the window and send it yourself.

The members table lists everyone with their workspace role, the **projects** they belong to (with their role in each project, or the **Full access** badge) and when they joined. The Projects column names only projects you can open and appears for people who manage members. Pending invitations are listed below the table: copy the link, send it again, invite again after it expired, or cancel it.

You need the right to add members to add an existing account, and the right to invite members to send an invitation. You can only give a workspace role with the same permissions as yours or fewer, and nobody can give the owner role.

<Warning>
  **Privacy and consent:** the search in **Add people** looks through all accounts of the server by name and email, also accounts that are in no workspace with you. It shows only the name, email and picture, at most 20 matches, and never guest accounts, banned accounts or people who are in the workspace already. Anybody who can add members can use it, and **adding needs no consent of the person**: they are in your workspace at once and only get a notification and an email. On a server where anybody can register and create a workspace, that means any user can see the name and email of every account and add accounts to their own workspace. Administrators of a server that is open to people who should not know about each other should set `DISABLE_USER_DIRECTORY=true` and/or `DISABLE_WORKSPACE_CREATION=true` (on Kaneo Cloud the search is off unless `ENABLE_USER_DIRECTORY=true`). Without the search you cannot add accounts to a workspace directly: people are invited by email instead, and workspace members can still be added to projects. See [environment variables](/core/installation/environment-variables).

  Guest accounts can never search accounts or add people. Nobody can add more than 30 people per 10 minutes. A workspace holds at most 100 members by default, which also limits accepting invitations.
</Warning>

### Invite a person you already track as a resource

A person you added under **Settings -> Workspace -> Resources** can get a Kaneo account without losing the tasks assigned to them:

1. Give the person resource an email address (edit it in the resources list).
2. Click **Invite**. Choose the workspace role and the projects (the projects with their tasks are ticked already), with a role in each. You can only give roles and project access that you hold yourself.
3. Send the invitation. If email is not configured on the server, copy the link from the dialog and send it yourself.
4. When the person accepts, the resource is linked to their account and its assignments in the projects they can open move to the account. If they already had an assignment on the same task, one assignment is kept, with the higher allocation. Assignments in projects they cannot open stay on the resource. The workload shows one row for the person.

If the person is already a member of the workspace, use **Link to member** instead. It needs the right to manage members. **Unlink** removes the link, but assignments that already moved stay with the member.

## 2. Control who can see which project

Access to a project comes from being a member of that project:

- Workspace **owners** and roles that can manage workspace settings (the built-in **admin** role) see every project in the workspace and act with their workspace role.
- Everyone else (built-in **member** and **viewer**, and custom roles) only sees the projects they were added to. Inside a project, the permissions of their project role apply, which can differ from their workspace role. `owner` is never a project role.
- Members only see data of their own projects: the project list, search, workload, activity, labels on tasks, relations, notifications and the member list leave out projects they were not added to.
- The person who creates a project becomes its admin, unless they already have full access to every project.
- A project member with the right permission can add other workspace members to the same project, but never with a role that has more permissions than their own.

<Warning>
  **Upgrading an existing installation:** workspace owners and roles that can manage workspace settings (such as the built-in admin) keep access to every existing project. All other existing workspace members (member, viewer and custom roles) lose access to all projects until an administrator adds them to the projects they need. If your workspace removed the permission to manage workspace settings from its admin role, those admins lose access as well until they are added.
</Warning>

### Project members and invitations

Manage who is in a project under **Settings -> Projects -> (choose the project) -> Members**.

**See who has access**

- The table lists everyone who can open the project and their role in the project.
- People with the **Full access** badge reach every project through their workspace role. You cannot change or remove them here. Change their workspace role under **Members** in the workspace.
- A member marked **inactive** has a role that gives no access any more, for example because the role was deleted. Choose a new role for them or remove them.

**Add people to the project**

1. Click **Add people**.
2. Type a name or an email address. Workspace members who are not in the project yet are listed right away.
3. Pick a person and choose their role in the project. Click **Add**.

If you are also allowed to add members to the workspace and the account search is on, the same field finds accounts that are not in the workspace yet. Then you choose the workspace role too, and **Add** puts the person in the workspace and in the project in one step. They are notified like any new member.

If nobody matches and you typed a full email address, choose **Send invitation to ...**, then choose the role in the workspace (it applies to the whole workspace) and the role in the project (only this project). If your server sends email, the person gets a message. If it does not, copy the invitation link from the window and send it yourself. The person opens the link, signs in or creates an account, and accepts. The invitation page shows the project and the role.

If the person is already in the workspace, Kaneo says so and offers **Add as member** instead.

The table shows each person's role in the project, their workspace role and when they joined the project.

**Follow up on invitations**

Pending invitations are listed below the members. For each one you can:

- copy the link;
- send it again, which also extends it by 48 hours;
- invite again, if it has expired (an expired invitation cannot be sent again; the window opens with the address and roles filled in);
- cancel it.

**Change or remove a member, or leave a project**

- Use the role menu in the table to change a role, and the actions menu to remove a person.
- To leave a project yourself, open your own row, click the actions menu and choose **Leave project**.

**Limits to know about**

- You can only give roles with the same permissions as yours or fewer. Roles you may not give do not appear in the lists.
- You only see the buttons your role allows. If Kaneo still refuses an action, it tells you why.
- A role cannot be deleted or renamed while project members or pending project invitations still use it. Give those people another role first.
- Someone who is in the workspace but not in any project sees the message "You do not have access to any project yet. Ask a workspace admin to add you to a project."

## 3. Assign clear ownership

For each active task:

- Assign one primary owner
- Use due dates for time-sensitive work
- Set priority to support tradeoff decisions

## 4. Standardize labels

Use labels to create a shared language across the team. Manage them at the workspace level in **Settings -> Workspace -> Labels** (see [Manage workspace labels](/core/functional/manage-workspace-labels)).

Examples:

- Work type: `bug`, `feature`, `chore`
- Domain: `api`, `web`, `infra`
- Risk/impact: `critical`, `customer-impact`

## 5. Use views intentionally

- **Board** for daily execution
- **List** for compact review and cleanup
- **Backlog** for planning and grooming

Switch views depending on the meeting or decision you are making.

## 6. Keep communication in the task

When scope, ownership, or timing changes, update the task directly so project history remains visible.
